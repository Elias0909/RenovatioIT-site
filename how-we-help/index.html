How a project runs

Five steps, no surprises.

I

A conversation

Video meeting with Tim Hopkins, tell him what is and isn't working. We will tell you honestly whether we're the right people, and roughly what it would cost.

II

A tailored live demo

To showcase what your new website could potentially look like. Recorded to show other parishioners what it could look like.

III

A written proposal

Finalise price and timeline before any work starts. One number, no hidden costs and a deadline we will work towards.

IV

Build and review

We build your website and keep you updated to review along the journey. Final review before website goes live.

V

Handover

Accounts, passwords and documentation in your name, plus training. You have full control over everything.
