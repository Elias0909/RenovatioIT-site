Before you write

Questions we're usually asked first.

What does it cost?

Projects are quoted as a fixed price after the first call, and ongoing support is a flat monthly fee. You'll have both in writing before anything starts.

We already have a website. Can you take it over?

Usually, yes. We'll review what's there, tell you whether it's worth keeping, and move it across if it is.

Nobody here is technical. Is that a problem?

No. We set things up so the person who writes the bulletin can update the site, and we train whoever will be doing it.

Do you only work with Catholic organisations?

That's who we're built for, and it's most of what we do. If you're a Catholic parish, order, school or charity, you're in the right place.
