The full story

How we helped SCM London.

Fr. Tibor had a post in his newsletter searching for website expertise for years without any luck. When we started working with them, their WordPress site had been compromised. Spam links were being injected into pages, administrator accounts had filled with hundreds of thousands of bot accounts, and the website was not mobile friendly.

We took a full copy, and rebuilt the site clean, and updated to the latest updated version of WordPress. Content that mattered came across: parish news, Mass times, the photo archive. The malware and open doors for the hackers were removed.

The new site is simpler to update, works properly on a phone, and is welcoming to new visitors.
