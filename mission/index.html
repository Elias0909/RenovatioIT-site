What we're for

The Church should not be badly served by its own tools.

For Catholics, by Catholics. We created Renovatio IT after realising that many Catholic churches and organisations had websites that were not up to standard. Many parishes were unable to meet this need which is where we wanted to offer a helping hand.

Too many websites and IT systems are either expensive and not built for the needs of the parish, or free/cheap and outdated. We think there is a third option: built well and priced honestly by people who care about the faith rather than just getting the job done.

Technology in service of the Church, not the other way round.

That means we build things that outlast us: standard tools, documented setups, and accounts in your name, ensuring that your website will continue to run smoothly for your growing parish without any down time or locked into a never-ending monthly subscription.

What we hold to

Four commitments.

Service

We work for the common good of the Church. Profit matters, but it is not our only measure of success. We want every Catholic parish to have reliable, up-to-date technology that helps it serve its people and fulfil its mission.

Reverence

The design, the language and the images should suit what they're representing. A parish site should not look like a software startup.

Human Dignity

We design technology around the people a parish serves, making information clear, accessible, and welcoming.

Simplicity

We remove unnecessary complexity and recommend solutions that parish staff and volunteers can actually use.
