How we started

It began with a parish that needed help.

We are practising Catholics who work in tech for a living.

We kept meeting the same problems outside work: parishes and Catholic organisations had old websites, which nobody could update, or relied on a volunteer who moved away years ago. When the Slovak Catholic Mission's site was attacked, we helped, and realised how many others were in the same position.

Renovatio IT exists to fix these issues and build something that lasts.
